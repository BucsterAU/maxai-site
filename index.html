MaXai

The friendly assistant behind LAPZ

MaXai is the assistant for LAPZ, the Australian car community platform at lapz.au. MaXai sends text messages to LAPZ members and founders, such as welcome messages, event reminders and account notices.

SMS from MaXai

  • Messages come from the sender name “MaXai”.
  • Members can reply STOP to opt out.
  • MaXai never asks for passwords or payment details by text.
